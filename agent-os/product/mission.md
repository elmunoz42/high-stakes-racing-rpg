# Product Mission

Working title: **Racing RPG** (placeholder; pick a real name before any public release).

## Problem

Arcade racers give you the thrill of the race but nothing to care about between
races. Car-culture RPGs and management sims give you the garage but the driving
is an afterthought. Nobody has fused a 16-bit style arcade racer with a living
paddock where the people you deal with (your mechanic, the parts counter, the
sponsor rep, your coach) are actual characters you can talk to about anything.

## Target Users

- Players who grew up on 16-bit and 32-bit arcade racers and want that feel in a
  browser tab, no install.
- Car enthusiasts who enjoy the build as much as the race: real street cars,
  real aftermarket parts, real prices, real trade-offs.
- Players who like character-driven RPGs and want conversations that are not
  dialogue trees.

## Solution

A browser game with two halves of **equal weight**:

1. **The race.** Retro pseudo-3D (sprite-scaled road) arcade racing in street
   cars, against AI rivals, on original circuits and street courses.
2. **The paddock.** At every race location you walk a side-scrolling town and
   talk to the people there. Every character is driven by LLM inference with a
   rich, funny, consistent personality, a memory of you, and real leverage over
   your career: they sell and fit parts, offer sponsorship deals, and coach you.

What makes it different:

- **Conversation has mechanical consequences.** You can haggle with the parts
  seller, talk a sponsor into a better deal, or annoy your mechanic into a
  slower job. The LLM proposes; deterministic game rules decide.
- **Real cars, real parts, real prices.** The car and parts catalogue is
  sourced real-world data (make, model, year, specs, part number, list price,
  source link, date checked), so a build in the game is a build you could
  price up in real life.
- **Personality first.** Characters are written as people with wants, quirks,
  grudges and running jokes, not as menu skins.

## Principles

- **RPG immersion is as important as the racing.** Neither half is a minigame
  for the other. Every milestone ships progress on both.
- **Original work in a loved genre.** The game takes inspiration from the feel
  of 90s arcade racers. It does not copy any existing game's name, tracks,
  characters, art, music or UI. All of those are original.
- **Facts, not logos.** Real cars and parts appear by name with factual specs
  and prices. No manufacturer logos, badges or official artwork; car art is
  original pixel art. Sponsors and teams in the fiction are invented.
- **The engine is the referee.** Money, inventory and car stats change only
  through validated game actions, never because a model said so.
