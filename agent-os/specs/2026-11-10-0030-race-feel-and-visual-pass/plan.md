# Race Feel and Visual Pass — Plan

## Task 1: Save spec documentation

This folder; stories US-010 and US-011.

## Task 2: Physics

Lateral inertia; grip limit with understeer scrub and a slide state;
drivetrain traits; handbrake; slipstream; gears, revs and shift cut; rumble
strips; two-car contact. Unit tests for each. Re-check race balance.

## Task 3: Race visuals and HUD

Canvas-drawn sky, parallax layers, ground themes, cars (steer frames, brake
lights), scenery, start gantry; smoke, dust and speed lines; speed-sensitive
camera; tachometer, gear, slipstream and lap-progress HUD.

## Task 4: Town visuals

Street, buildings, sea and sky; characters with walk frames; interiors;
drawn portraits in the dialogue panel.

## Task 5: Play-check and tune

Drive it in a browser, tune constants, compare screenshots.

## Final Task: Verify stories

Verify US-010 and US-011 and re-run US-001 to US-009 at demo level.
